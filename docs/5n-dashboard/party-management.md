# Party Management

The "Parties" page lists the parties your participant knows. Local parties, the ones hosted on your participant, have a check in the "Local" column. Turn on "Show only Local Parties" to hide the others, or search by any part of a party ID. Click an ID to copy it.

## Allocate party

Click "Allocate Party", enter a hint (letters, numbers and hyphens) and click "Allocate". The new party ID is the hint followed by your participant's namespace, e.g. `dave::1220...`
